Features

📚

Track Everything

Keep a record of the books you read, TV shows and movies you watch, and podcasts you listen to, all in one place. Organize by shelves like To Read, Reading, Read, To Watch, Watching, and Watched.

💬

Share Recommendations

Send personalized recommendations to friends with messages explaining why they'll love a title. See who recommended what to you and keep track of every suggestion.

📺

Streaming & Trailers

Quickly see where a show or movie is streaming with icons right on the title. Watch trailers and browse the full cast without leaving the app.

🎬

Explore by Cast & Crew

Tap on any actor or director to explore their full filmography. Find every movie a person directed or acted in and add new discoveries to your shelves.

👥

Clubs

Create or join clubs: book clubs, movie groups, podcast circles. Share recommendations with a group and discover what others are enjoying.

⚔️

Rank Battle

Compare two titles side-by-side and pick your favorite. The Battle AI helps you build a ranked list of everything you've consumed, no more agonizing over your top 10.

📬

Inbox

View all incoming recommendations, club activity, and messages in one feed. Never miss a suggestion from a friend again.

🔍

Powerful Search

Search across books, movies, TV shows, and podcasts. Find titles quickly and add them to your shelves with a tap.

📋

Ordered & Ranked Shelves

Order your lists to decide what to read, watch, or listen to next. Switch between ordered and ranked views to see your personal rankings.

📝

Personal Notes

Add your own notes to any title, jot down thoughts, favorite moments, or why you loved (or didn't love) something.

How to Use ranxt

🚀

Getting Started

When you first open the app, you'll be prompted to create an account using your phone number. This will be your username and how people will connect with you. The display name entered is what will be sent along with any recommendations you send.


Once you're signed in, you'll be taken to your home screen. If you have received recommendations, you can see them in your inbox and take action on them by clicking on the titles.


To start tracking what you read, watch, and listen to, use the search box to find the titles. Type in the name of a book, movie, TV show, or podcast. After selecting the title, you can add it to your shelves to track if it's something you have already read, something you want to watch, etc.


Once you've added titles to your own shelves, you can share your recommendations with your friends by clicking on "Recommend" within each title.

👥

Clubs

Are you already in a book or movie club? Do you regularly share recommendations with a group of people? With ranxt you have the ability to create a club and invite your friends to join or accept an invitation to a friend's club.


Once you're in a Club, you can start sharing your own recommendations, and connecting with other members. It's easy to keep track of what you've watched or read and what you want to see or read next.


Whether you're a member of a Book Club, Movie Club, TV Show Club, or Podcast Club, ranxt is the perfect platform for tracking and sharing recommendations with others who share your passions. So why wait? Join a Club today and start exploring the world of books, movies, TV shows, and podcasts with other like-minded enthusiasts!

⚔️

Battle

Looking for a fun and easy way to compare your favorite books, movies, TV shows, or podcasts? Look no further than Rank Battle! This unique feature of the ranxt app allows you to compare two titles side-by-side and vote on your favorite.


Battle is a great way to see how your titles stack up against each other. And the best part? The more you Battle, the smarter the system gets. The app keeps track of which titles you like best, so your list is always sorted.


To get started, go to the Battle page in the app. You can switch between media tabs, and then compare titles by dragging them to "Liked A Lot More" or "Liked A Little More." It's as easy as that and ranxt does the rest. Go to your Read, Watched, and Listened to Shelves to see how your titles stack up against each other.
