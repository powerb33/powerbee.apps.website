Skull King Scorer has moved!

Skull King scoring is now part of Ultimate Game Scorer, our all-in-one game night companion. Choose from standard, rascal, rascal with cannons, +14 cards, pirate abilities, loot, and more. Customize card counts, skip to the brawl, Whirlpool, it's all there. The app saves every game so you can dive into stats by game, by player, or head-to-head. It also includes a custom Flip 7 keyboard and lets you build your own game types. Free games are included to try it out, with ads or a subscription to keep going.
